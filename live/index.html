        About the data

        Footage. Indexed clips from the team's VAST archive. Use the Set menu: Industrial is warehouse and indoor facility clips from the event corpus (rendered scenes). Hackathon is team footage for the violation ruleset. Streets is highway and street cameras. Replay plays recorded segments; it is not a live camera.

        Always running. People: YOLO11 boxes recorded at ingest for every frame. Scene captions: the description Cosmos Reason wrote for each 5-second segment.

        Your rules. W&B Inference maps your words to modules in the library; the server rejects anything outside it. Cosmos Reason proposes the forklift lane from a camera frame. Lane, lingering, and crowding rules are then computed in your browser from the real person boxes. "Near a forklift" is a VAST hybrid search over captions. Its score ranks relevance; it is not a safety confidence.

        Limits. The detector does not box forklifts, so forklift rules rely on captions. Positions are in image space, not calibrated metres. Flagged moments are for human review, not compliance findings.

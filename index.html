ABOUT THE DEMO

Real footage. Transparent preview.

Three recorded views from one factory demonstrate the configuration and evidence workflow. Rules and thresholds are functional; event intervals and overlays are illustrative, manually authored examples.

No object detector or language model is connected. No footage is uploaded. A source “safe” label does not establish whole-scene safety or OSHA compliance.

Reference clips show a contrasting source example; they are not footage corrected by AI. Zone geometry is illustrative and requires calibration before real use.
